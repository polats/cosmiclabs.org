Privacy Policy

Game-A-Day — Last updated: March 23, 2026

Game-A-Day is built by Cosmic Labs and runs as a Devvit app inside Reddit. We believe in collecting as little data as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's stored.

1. What We Collect

Game-A-Day stores a small amount of data to make the games work. Here's everything:

Data Purpose Stored Where
Reddit username Identify you on leaderboards and save your progress Reddit (Devvit Redis)
Game scores Leaderboard rankings Reddit (Devvit Redis)
Game state Save/resume progress (level, data) Reddit (Devvit Redis)
Play streak Track consecutive days played Reddit (Devvit Redis)
Votes & proposals Community game voting Reddit (Devvit Redis)

That's it. We do not collect:

  • Email addresses or real names
  • Location or IP address data
  • Device information or fingerprints
  • Analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment or financial information (the Service is free)

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored using Reddit's Devvit Redis, which is Reddit's server-side key-value store for apps. We do not operate our own servers or databases. Data lives within Reddit's infrastructure and is subject to Reddit's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to make Game-A-Day work:

  • Display your scores on leaderboards
  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Track your play streak
  • Record your votes and game proposals
  • Show moderator status (for mod-only features)

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond the Service's core functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Your data stays within Reddit's infrastructure. The only public data is:

  • Your username and score on leaderboards (visible to other players)
  • Your game proposals and votes (visible to the community)

5. Cookies & Tracking

Game-A-Day does not use cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or any third-party tracking tools. The games run inside Reddit's webview and do not load external scripts beyond the game engine itself (GameMaker or Godot runtime).

6. Children's Privacy

Game-A-Day is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since the Service runs inside Reddit, users must meet Reddit's minimum age requirement. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete their game data.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • View your data — your scores, streak, and game state are visible in the app
  • Delete your data — contact us (see below) and we'll remove your game data from Redis
  • Stop using the Service — simply stop playing; we don't send notifications or emails

If you're in the EU/EEA, California, or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you have additional rights including access, correction, portability, and erasure. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Data Retention

Game data is retained as long as the Service is active on the subreddit. If the app is uninstalled or discontinued, all data stored in Devvit Redis is deleted by Reddit. We do not maintain separate backups of player data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if we change what data we collect or how we use it. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. We don't expect significant changes — our goal is to keep data collection minimal.
